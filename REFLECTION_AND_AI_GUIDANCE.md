# Reflection and AI acknowledgement guidance

The detailed submission requirement says 500–1000 words, 1–2 pages. The overview's “500–100” is inconsistent; use the detailed requirement unless your lecturer confirms otherwise.

Write about your own experience using Gibbs' six stages. Suggested allocation:
1. Description (100–150 words): app purpose, tasks you actually completed, and architecture.
2. Feelings (75–100): your real reactions while learning Compose, Retrofit and debugging.
3. Evaluation (100–150): what worked and failed, supported by actual tests.
4. Analysis (150–200): explain why separating UI, state and networking helped; discuss a real challenge.
5. Conclusion (75–100): what you learned and what remains uncertain.
6. Action plan (100–150): concrete next steps such as localisation, physical-device accessibility testing or background refresh.

Do not state that you ran tests, made frequent commits, or solved an issue unless you did. No completed personal reflection is supplied because your experience must come from you.
